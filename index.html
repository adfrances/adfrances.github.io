Hello! I am a Geography PhD student at The Ohio State University and a Health Places Index (HPI) Data Advisor at the Public Health Alliance of Southern California. My work focuses on spatial inequities in housing, health, and accessibility, using data-driven approaches to support more inclusive and equitable communities.
